Scope of Practice

Last updated: 1 September 2026

What this is

Compassion Key coaching is a complementary wellbeing practice. It is not psychotherapy or medical care, and is not a substitute for treatment from a qualified health professional.

This isn't the right fit if:

you're in acute crisis, experiencing active suicidal thoughts, or need a diagnosis. Coaching complements professional care — it doesn't replace it. If you're in crisis in Canada, call or text 988 (Talk Suicide Canada) any time, or call 911.

Who it's for

Compassion Key sessions can help with fears, anxiety, PTSD symptoms, trauma, ADHD and neurodivergent overwhelm, scarcity patterns, and relationship patterns — worked on as a named issue, without needing to retell the full story.

Credentials

Shri Vivek is a Certified Compassion Key Practitioner, Accredited Enneagram Practitioner, and Therapeutic Life Coach. These are coaching-practice credentials, not clinical licenses.
