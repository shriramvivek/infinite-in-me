Scope of Practice

Last updated: 5 September 2026

What this is

Sound Healing, Access Bars, and Compassion Key are complementary wellness practices. None of them is psychotherapy or medical care, and none is a substitute for treatment from a qualified health professional.

This isn't the right fit if:

you're in acute crisis, experiencing active suicidal thoughts, or need a diagnosis. These sessions complement professional care — they don't replace it. If you're in crisis in Canada, call or text 988 (Talk Suicide Canada) any time, or call 911.

Who it's for

People come to these sessions for stress, a busy mind, fear or worry that won't switch off, old hurt that still stings, money stress, or strain with people they love — worked on gently, without needing to retell the full story. If you're already being treated for a diagnosed condition, these sessions are meant to sit alongside your care team, not replace it.

Credentials
