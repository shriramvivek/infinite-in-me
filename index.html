What to expect

Your journey into silence

More than a session — a guided passage out of the noise of the world and into a deep quiet. Each journey unfolds in four movements.

i

The Threshold

You cross out of ordinary time. Shoes off, breath softening, the space held in candlelight and the warm glow of Himalayan salt. Nothing is asked of you but to arrive.

ii

Attunement

We talk for a few minutes. I ask what your week's been like and what your body's doing. Then I pick the modality, instruments and frequencies your body is asking for.

iii

The Work

Waves of sound, the quiet release of the bars, or the guided compassion process carry you beneath thought — into that deep quiet, where healing begins.

iv

The Return

You surface slowly, wrapped in quiet. A sip of water, a few grounding words, and the calm — with its clarity — is yours to carry back into the world.

The Science of Sound

Why vibration heals

Sound is not only heard — it is felt. When precisely tuned frequencies move through the body, the nervous system responds in measurable ways.

Stress reduction

Sound therapy is studied for lowering cortisol and activating the parasympathetic nervous system — the state in which repair happens.

Sleep & mood

Research links sound meditation with improved sleep quality, lower tension and improved mood — a felt shift that can begin in a single session.
