Access Bars

32 points, one quiet mind

Access Bars is a light touch technique using 32 points on the head, each connected to an area of life — money, creativity, control, ageing and more. As each point is gently held, the electromagnetic charge tied to old thoughts, judgments and fixed beliefs starts to release.

Most people describe it as one of the most relaxing things they've ever experienced — many fall asleep during the session.

Access Bars is a complementary wellness practice. It is not medical or psychological treatment and does not replace care from your doctor or therapist — see our scope of practice.
