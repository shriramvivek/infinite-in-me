The Method

What is the Compassion Key?

The Compassion Key doesn't manage your pain.
It finds the source — and dissolves it.

Developed by Edward Mannix after years of study with a hidden master in England, the Compassion Key has touched tens of thousands of lives around the world. It is elegantly simple: after a short, powerful meditation, we gently delve into the issue together. Guided statements direct self-compassion to the exact origin of the suffering — and the emotional charge is followed until it is nullified.

No retelling your story for years. No techniques to practice daily. Just awareness, emotion — and transformation.

The Research

Compassion, studied

The Compassion Key is a coaching process — and the healing power of directed compassion is an active field of clinical research.

A meta-analysis of 21 randomized controlled trials found compassion-based interventions produced significant improvements in depression, anxiety, psychological distress and well-being.

Kirby, Tellegen & Steindl, Behavior Therapy, 2017. View on PubMed →

An early systematic review of compassion-focused therapy reported promising outcomes, particularly for people high in shame and self-criticism.

Leaviss & Uttley, Psychological Medicine, 2015. View on PubMed →

Compassion Key coaching is a complementary wellbeing practice. It is not psychotherapy or medical care, and is not a substitute for treatment from a qualified health professional.

Questions

Before you begin

What is the Compassion Key?+

A coaching process developed by Edward Mannix. After a short, powerful meditation, guided statements direct self-compassion to the exact source of an issue, and the emotional charge is followed until it is nullified. Awareness + Emotion = Transformation.

How many sessions will I need?+

Most people experience real change within 1–3 targeted sessions — many feel a shift within hours of their first. Deeper, older traumas may take a little longer.

Is it safe?+

Yes. The process uses only your own awareness and emotions — nothing external — and you remain in control throughout. It is gentle even when the material is heavy. It complements, but does not replace, professional medical or psychological care.

Does it work online?+

Fully. Sessions over video call are just as effective — Shri works with clients worldwide online, and in person in Mississauga, Ontario.

What can it help with?+

Fears, anxiety, PTSD, deep-seated trauma, ADHD and neurodivergent overwhelm, scarcity and money blocks, self-worth, and resetting relationships — practically anything with an emotional root.
