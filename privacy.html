Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Draft — pending legal review. This describes current practice in plain language; it is not a substitute for professional legal advice.

Who this covers

This policy applies to soundwaystohealth.com and to anyone who books, contacts, or attends a Sound Healing, Access Bars, or Compassion Key session with Shri Vivek ("Soundways to Health," "we," "us").

What we collect

  • Booking details. Name, email, phone number, and session preferences, collected through our website form when you request a session.
  • Payment information. Processed directly by our booking/payment platform. We do not receive or store your full card details. If you pay by Interac e-Transfer, we see only the sender name and email tied to the transfer.
  • Session content. What you share during a session is held in confidence and is not recorded or shared unless you ask us to, or the law requires it.
  • Email correspondence. If you email us, we keep that correspondence to respond to you and for our records.

Where it's stored

Booking and contact data is stored by our website host and in our email account. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Third parties

Our website host processes booking-request forms on our behalf under its own privacy and security terms. Payments are arranged directly after availability is confirmed. If a session is held by video call, the video platform used (e.g. Zoom or Google Meet) processes the call under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and correspondence records for as long as needed to provide services, respond to you, and meet our own bookkeeping and legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email shriramvivek@gmail.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.
