Compassion Key

Reaching the root, gently

Compassion Key is a guided process developed by Edward Mannix. Through a series of gentle, specific questions, it helps trace a current fear or hurt back to where it first took hold — and lets your own compassion, not force, transform it.

You stay fully present and in control throughout. Nothing is done to you; you're guided through a process you direct.

Compassion Key is a complementary wellness practice, not psychotherapy or medical treatment. It is not appropriate for acute crisis or active suicidal thoughts — see our scope of practice. If you're in crisis in Canada, call or text 988 any time.
