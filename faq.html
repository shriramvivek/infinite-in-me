Things people worry about

Before you book, here's what most people want to know.

Do I have to talk about anything?

No. For sound healing and the head-touch session you don't have to say a word past hello. For compassion work we do talk, and you decide how much.

Will you touch me?

Only in the head session, and only lightly, on your head. I'll tell you before I do. You stay fully clothed the whole time, in everything I offer.

What do I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable lying down in. Bring socks — feet get cold.

What if I cry, or fall asleep, or nothing happens?

All three are normal and all three are fine. Sleeping doesn't mean it didn't work. Nothing happening the first time doesn't mean nothing will.

Can I stop?

Any moment. You say stop, we stop. You don't owe me a reason.

Is this instead of my doctor or therapist?

No. It sits beside them. If you're being treated for something, keep being treated for it. Tell your care team you're doing this — most are glad to hear it.

What if I think this is nonsense?

Then come for the ninety minutes of quiet. Plenty of people have.
