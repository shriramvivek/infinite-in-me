Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Rescheduling

Need to move your session? Email shriramvivek@gmail.com or use the reschedule link in your booking confirmation at least 24 hours before your appointment, and we'll find a new time at no extra charge.

Cancellation notice

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your session for a full refund or free reschedule. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the session are non-refundable, but may be credited toward a future session at the practitioner's discretion.

No-shows

If you miss a scheduled session without giving notice, it's treated as a completed session and is non-refundable.

Refunds

If Infinite In Me needs to cancel or reschedule a session, you'll receive a full refund or a free reschedule to a time that works for you — your choice. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

How to cancel

Email shriramvivek@gmail.com or call (647) 376-6687.
