About Shri Vivek

Sound healing, Access Bars and Compassion Key facilitator in Mississauga, Ontario.

Why I do this work

A practice built from my own search for calm

For years I looked for something that actually helped me feel calmer and more like myself — most of it didn't. Sound did. So did compassion work. That's why I trained in this — I know what it's like to sit in a room hoping something will finally help.

I've sat with a lot of people through hard moments, and I've learned that healing rarely comes from trying harder. It comes from finally being still enough to let something shift.

Trained and certified in: Compassion Key (certified practitioner) · Enneagram (accredited practitioner) · life coaching · NLP · yoga · Himalayan sound practice

Shri Vivek offers complementary wellness practices. These credentials are not clinical or regulated health-profession licenses under Ontario's Regulated Health Professions Act — see our scope of practice.
