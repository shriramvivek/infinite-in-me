Sound Healing

A wash of sound, felt in the body

You lie down, fully clothed, and the sound moves through and around you — Himalayan bowls, crystal bowls, gongs, tuning forks, handpan. Nothing is asked of you. Your only job is to receive.

Sessions are shaped around what your body needs that day: deep rest, a nervous-system reset, or simply an hour where nothing is required of you.

Sound healing is a complementary wellness practice. It is not medical or psychological treatment and does not replace care from your doctor or therapist — see our scope of practice.
